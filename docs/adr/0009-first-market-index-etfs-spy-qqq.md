# 0009. First market is index ETFs, starting with SPY and QQQ

**Status**: Accepted
**Date**: 2026-09-18

## Context
The intraday momentum evidence is peer reviewed and specific to index ETFs. Their spreads are the tightest in the market, which matters most on a small account, and they need no stock-picking code.

## Decision
The approved list starts with SPY and QQQ only.

## Consequences
Adding a symbol is a loosening: approved list, backtest, paper, then live.
